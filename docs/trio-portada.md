# Ilustración conjunta de portada

Se sustituye la composición de tres sprites independientes por una única ilustración de los tres amigos. El dibujo de Adri es la referencia de estilo: contornos, sombreado y proporciones compartidos. Los tres aparecen de frente, con la misma altura visible y los pies alineados, conservando sus rasgos y prendas.

Generación integrada (built-in), con las tres ilustraciones existentes como referencias. Recurso: `public/assets/characters/amigos-comic-unificado-v1.png`. Los recursos individuales de cuentos y avatares se conservan.

## Prompt final

Use case: identity-preserve / illustration-story. Create ONE unified transparent comic illustration of THREE six-year-old school friends standing side by side, full body. Reference 1 is Adri, reference 2 Juan, reference 3 Cayetana. Exact left-to-right order: Adri, Juan, Cayetana. Preserve EACH child's distinct facial likeness and hair, especially Cayetana's cheerful toothy smile from her reference. Keep outfits: Adri navy/orange jacket and navy trousers with backpack; Juan pale blue shirt with navy/white sleeves, navy trousers and blue sneakers with backpack; Cayetana pastel lilac/mint leaf print knee-length dress, pink shoes and lilac backpack. UNIFY THE STYLE: all three drawn by the same comic artist using ADRI reference 1 as the master style: bold dark ink contours, warm cel/painterly shading, same face rendering and line weight, consistent detail, natural six-year-old proportions. Crucial: all THREE children have THE SAME VISIBLE HEIGHT head-to-shoe, same head size and body scale, same eye-line and ground baseline, viewed from same distance. No child smaller, taller, farther back, or more realistic. Each about 4.5 to 5 heads tall, with natural young-child bodies. Friendly relaxed upright poses, Adri and Juan thumbs up, Cayetana one hand waving below top of head. Stand in a horizontal row with a small clear gap between silhouettes, no overlap. All heads and both shoes fully visible. Equal even spacing. The entire group fills the canvas with only narrow safe transparent margins. Truly transparent alpha outside silhouettes, no scenery, no background, no gradient halo, no floor, no cast shadow, no text, name labels, border, watermark or additional people. Final is a coherent cast illustration for an educational app homepage, NOT a collage of three unmatched source sprites.
